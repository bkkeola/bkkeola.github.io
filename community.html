Culinary arts · Community leadership · Hawaiʻi

Food as a force for good

Every opportunity to serve has helped me grow as a culinary student, a leader and a member of my community.

Giving back where I began

My Sister’s Keeper — Culinary Fundraiser with Purpose. I volunteered with my mentor, Chef Lee Shinsato, to support a fundraiser connected to the WCCC culinary program and women and children in need. I helped serve food and encouraged fellow culinary students to participate. It was a meaningful full-circle experience.

Alani Children’s Center · Gingerbread house holiday event

I developed and coordinated the event from planning through execution, bringing together volunteers, culinary instructors and community support to create a joyful holiday experience for keiki and families. I learned to manage logistics, communicate with partners and make an event inclusive.

Kona Low Flood Relief Bake Sale

I helped organize and lead a community bake sale on May 1, 2026, bringing students and community members together to support families affected by flooding. This experience demonstrated how baking, partnership and service can respond to immediate community needs.

Culinary Institute of the Pacific & KCC service

I volunteered with Ms. Lauren in food innovation, Department of Education events, SPAM JAM® and administrative support. I also supported a Celebration of Life event with Chef Garnier, volunteered with The Pantry and assisted Mr. Santa at Ka ʻIkena events. Each opportunity strengthened my adaptability, hospitality and collaboration.

Service & Sustainability Learning Program

At KCC, I coordinate service-learning opportunities, volunteers, student outreach, website content and event logistics. My work has included community and sustainability initiatives, youth outreach, domestic violence awareness and support for student learning. I also shared my culinary student experience at a counselor workshop.
